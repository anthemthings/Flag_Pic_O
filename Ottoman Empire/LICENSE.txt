# Ottoman flags 1920x1080

All ottoman flags in 1920x1080 resolution.

## Source
All flags sourced from Wikimedia Commons (Public Domain).
Edited to 1920x1080 resolution using Paint.NET.
Ottoman Empire (1499-1514) flag is derived from Ottoman Empire (1514-1517) flag.
